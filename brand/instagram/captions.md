## Tips while posting

- **Alt text** (Advanced settings → Accessibility → Write alt text): describe the first slide, for example "Illustrated Punjabi suit design concepts in arches with the title Worldwide Shipping Available".
- **Location:** leave it empty, because there is no physical boutique.
- **Music:** carousels can have soft instrumental music. Avoid songs with lyrics you don't have rights to.
- **Honesty:** every picture is labelled "Ruhaniat Design Concept". When you stitch real outfits, post real photos with the customer's permission, and only call something a customer order if it is one.
- **Reply quickly** to every comment and DM; at the start that matters more than posting often.
